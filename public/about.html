About Us

UNI Events

Your premier platform for discovering and managing university events across Sri Lanka

Our Mission

UNI Events is dedicated to connecting students across Sri Lankan universities with exciting events, competitions, workshops, and cultural activities. We aim to create a vibrant community where students can discover, participate in, and organize events that enrich their university experience.

What We Offer

  • ✓ Event Discovery: Browse through a wide range of events from music festivals to coding competitions
  • ✓ Easy Registration: Book events with just a few clicks, with support for both free and paid events
  • ✓ Volunteer Opportunities: Apply to volunteer at events and gain valuable experience
  • ✓ Event Requests: Suggest events you'd like to see organized
  • ✓ Secure Platform: Verified student accounts ensure a safe and trusted environment

Get Involved

Whether you're a student looking to attend events, an organizer planning an event, or someone with event ideas, UNI Events is here for you. Join our community and be part of the vibrant university event scene in Sri Lanka.
